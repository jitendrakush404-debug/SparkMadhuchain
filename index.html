The Problem

KVIC's Honey Mission faces critical challenges that affect both farmers and consumers

🚫 77% Brands Failed NMR Test

CSE 2020 investigation — 10 of 13 tested honey brands failed purity test. Consumer trust destroyed.

❌ No Bottle-Level Verification

No way to verify authenticity at bottle level — QR alone can be cloned

📋 Weak Market Linkages

Farmers earn ~₹85/kg to middlemen vs ₹400/kg retail — huge income gap

🔍 No Farm-to-Table Traceability

Impossible to track honey journey. Only 14,859 beekeepers on Madhukranti (PIB, Oct 2025)

Our Solution

An integrated Blockchain + AI + Weather-based digital ecosystem that revolutionizes honey traceability

About MadhuChain

Revolutionizing honey traceability with blockchain technology

🎯🌟 Our Mission

To create a transparent, trustworthy, and efficient honey supply chain that benefits both farmers and consumers while eliminating counterfeit products from the market.

By leveraging Hyperledger Fabric blockchain, self-hosted AI (Ollama + Qwen 2.5), and weather-based hive monitoring, we're building a future where every drop of honey can be traced back to its origin — with dual-key bottle verification that is practically impossible to counterfeit.
