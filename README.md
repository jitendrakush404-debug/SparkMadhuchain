---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- 77% honey brands in India fail NMR purity test
- Beekeepers earn ₹85/kg vs ₹400/kg retail price
- Zero bottle-level traceability exists
- Rural farmers exploited by middlemen daily

---

## 💡 Our Solution
A ₹0/month decentralized ecosystem offering:
- ✅ Dual-Key Anti-Counterfeit (QR + Secret Cap PIN)
- ✅ Hyperledger Fabric Blockchain Traceability
- ✅ AI Advisory in Hindi via Telegram Bot
- ✅ Bee Credits → Direct DBT to Farmers
- ✅ ₹20 Consumer Cashback per verified bottle
